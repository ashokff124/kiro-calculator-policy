KIRO Calculator

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how KIRO Calculator handles information when you use the application. It is based on the application source code reviewed for this release. Android packaging, native permissions and deployment configuration were not included in that review.

2. Calculation history and local storage

KIRO Calculator stores calculation history in browser or app WebView local storage on your device. The reviewed history module retains up to 500 entries. Entries may include calculator name, category, expression, result and timestamp.

The reviewed history code does not send this history to a cloud service. You can use Clear History to remove saved calculation history. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app may also remove locally stored information.

3. Fonts and external websites

The app requests DM Sans and Manrope fonts from Google Fonts. Google may receive ordinary connection information when serving these fonts. External protocol and publication websites open only when you select their links and are governed by their own privacy policies.

4. Analytics and cloud services

No analytics, advertising, Firebase or Supabase SDK dependency was identified in the supplied package configuration and source files reviewed. This finding is limited to the files reviewed and is not a guarantee about separately configured Android or hosting components.

5. Support

If you contact support, information in your email will be handled by your email provider and our support mailbox. Please do not include confidential or patient-identifiable information.

Contact: kiro.support.official@gmail.com

6. Changes

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change.
